Choose how hands-on you’d like to be.

You can leave the ongoing technical care with us, or keep control of hosting and maintenance yourself. Either way, the arrangement and any third-party costs are clear before you commit.

Managed by Lookout

Choose a monthly managed plan and we’ll take care of agreed services such as hosting, routine updates, backups, and ongoing support. We’ll confirm the plan, monthly fee, and any third-party costs before you commit.

Use a hosting provider and accounts you control. We’ll hand over the site and access, and you can pay your provider directly and look after hosting and updates yourself. You can also arrange support separately when needed.
